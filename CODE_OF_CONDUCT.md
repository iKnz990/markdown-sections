# Code of Conduct

This project adapts the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. To report a concern, [open an issue](https://github.com/iKnz990/markdown-sections/issues) asking the maintainer for a private contact channel. Do not include names, personal information, allegations, or evidence in that public request. Share those details only after a private channel is established. For software vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
